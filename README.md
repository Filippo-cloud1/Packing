# Packing

**A simple counter app for parachute riggers and packers.**

Packing helps you keep track of how many parachutes you pack, for whom, and how much each client owes you — right from your phone, even without an internet connection.

👉 **Open the app:** https://filippo-cloud1.github.io/Packing/

---

## Features

- **Daily counter** – Start each day empty, add the clients you're packing for, and tap their tile to count +1 pack. Made a mistake? Tap − to undo.
- **Earnings at a glance** – See today's packs and the money due, with a default price per pack (e.g. €6) and custom prices per client or per single pack.
- **Summaries** – Totals per client by day, week, month or all time, plus a monthly earnings chart.
- **Client ranking** – A podium of your top clients by earnings; tap a client to see packs, working days, average per day, amount owed and trend vs the previous period.
- **Payments & "black list"** – Record payments with method (cash, bank transfer, PayPal, etc.) and instantly see who still owes you money.
- **Tips** – Extra money is tracked separately as tips, with monthly, yearly and total counters.
- **Calendar** – Mark your working days, add notes, and send them to Google Calendar or any phone calendar (.ics).
- **Notes** – Permanent notes per client (canopy, preferences, agreements) and daily notes per client.
- **Italian & English** – Switch language anytime in Settings; dates and currency follow the chosen language.
- **Settings** – Your name, default price, payment methods, light/dark theme, accent color, vibration, double-tap protection and backup reminders.
- **Backup & restore** – Send a backup file to yourself by email or save it to Drive, and restore it on a new phone.

## Install on your phone

No app store needed.

- **Android:** open the link above in **Chrome** → menu ⋮ → **Install app** (or *Add to Home screen*).
- **iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.

The app then opens full screen like a normal app and works offline.

## Privacy

All data (clients, packs, payments, notes) is stored **only on your device**. Nothing is sent to a server, and no account is required. Each person who installs the app has their own separate data.

Because data lives on the phone, remember to make a backup from **Settings → Send backup** from time to time.

## Updates

When a new version is published here, the app updates itself the next time you open it with an internet connection. The current version is shown at the bottom of the Settings page.

## Tech

A lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA): a single HTML file with plain JavaScript and CSS, a web app manifest and a service worker for offline use. Hosted for free on GitHub Pages.
